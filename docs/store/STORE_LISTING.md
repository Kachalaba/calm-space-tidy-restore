## Full description (4000 chars max)

### EN

```
An old family workshop is waiting. Dust on the window, boxes in the walkway,
a drawer that never closes properly. Nothing here is broken beyond repair —
it just needs someone with a quiet hour.

Calm Space is a tidying game with no pressure of any kind. There is no timer.
There is no way to lose. Nothing chases you, nothing expires, and nothing
asks you to come back tomorrow.

You restore the workshop one small zone at a time. Sort the trail stones onto
their shelf. Bring order to the paint jars. Free the passage to the workbench.
Each finished task changes that corner of the room permanently — and the room
stays changed, every time you open the app.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Eight hand-painted restoration tasks
• Each one takes a few quiet minutes
• Finish a task and watch that part of the room come back to life
• Progress is permanent — the workshop remembers

WHAT YOU WILL NOT FIND
• No timers or countdowns
• No fail states, no lives, no energy
• No forced ads or pop-up interruptions
• No account, no sign-up
• No internet connection required

THE ART
Every surface of the workshop was painted for this game — warm gouache,
visible paper grain, sage and clay and cream, morning light through the
window. No stock assets.

LANGUAGES
English, Українська, Русский

Calm Space is a small, complete demo of a longer restoration. If you like the
quiet of it, more rooms are coming.
```
