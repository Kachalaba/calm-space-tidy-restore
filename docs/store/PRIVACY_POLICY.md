# Privacy Policy — Calm Space: Tidy & Restore

**Last updated: 8 August 2026**

## The short version

Calm Space: Tidy & Restore does not collect, store, or share any personal
data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics services, no
third-party SDKs, and no network requests. The game works entirely offline.

## What the app stores

The game saves one file on your own device containing your progress: which
restoration stages you have completed, your in-game token balance, your chosen
language, and your sound setting. This file never leaves your device, is not
readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

We cannot see this file. We have no server that receives it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account.
- It does not request location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
- It does not contain advertising and does not show ads.
- It does not contain in-app purchases.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or attribution services.
- It does not connect to the internet at all.
- It does not use cookies or any tracking technology.

## Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions.

## Children

The app is safe for all ages in its content, but it is not directed at
children specifically. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, no
personal information is gathered from anyone, including children.

## Data deletion

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete.
Uninstalling the app removes the saved progress file from your device.

## Changes

If a future version of the app introduces anything that collects data — for
example advertising or online features — this policy will be updated before
that version is released, and the change will be described here.
