Privacy Policy

Calm Space: Tidy & Restore — last updated 8 August 2026

Calm Space: Tidy & Restore does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no network requests. The game works entirely offline.

What the app stores

The game saves one file on your own device containing your progress: which restoration stages you have completed, your in-game token balance, your chosen language, and your sound setting. This file never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app. We have no server that receives it and no way to read it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account.
  • It does not request location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • It contains no advertising and shows no ads.
  • It contains no in-app purchases.
  • It uses no analytics, crash reporting, or attribution services.
  • It does not connect to the internet at all.
  • It uses no cookies or tracking technology.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions.

Children

The content is suitable for all ages, but the app is not specifically directed at children. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, no personal information is gathered from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes the saved progress file.

Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces anything that collects data — for example advertising or online features — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
